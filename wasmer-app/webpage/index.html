01 · Curated accessories

Useful.
Selected.
Worth buying.

Instead of hundreds of random products, the shop focuses on accessories that are genuinely useful, look good and make sense for the vehicle. The idea is to filter the market for the customer.

Example: mounts, storage, detailing, cockpit accessories, smart add-ons.
02 · Premium upgrades

Better quality.
Less compromise.

Premium items are tested, inspected and selected before they are offered. Higher-end products can be sold through pre-orders or small batches instead of filling the store with cheap generic alternatives.

Example: premium phone mounts, lighting systems, electronics, model-specific upgrades.
03 · Professional installation

Buy it.
Have it fitted.

Selected products can be purchased together with installation. The customer arrives, the product is ready, and the final result is clean, tested and properly integrated into the vehicle.

Example: hidden wiring, USB power, cockpit integration, lighting, trackers, mounts.
04 · Custom engineering

Can't find it?
Build it.

The custom lab is where electrical knowledge, programming, CAD, 3D printing and fabrication become the real difference. A one-off customer problem can become a prototype — and eventually a real product.

Example: custom brackets, sensor modules, BLE controls, displays, vehicle-specific parts.

Terms & Conditions

Draft · Version 2026-09-07

Complete before public launch.

The operator’s legal name, full postal address, shipping areas and costs must still be added and the finished terms should be reviewed for the countries where Wobli sells.

1. About Wobli

Wobli is a project for 3D-printed objects. The legal operator, contact details and full business address will be added before launch.

2. Orders and payment

Online ordering and payment are not yet available. These terms will be completed when a payment provider and fulfilment process are configured.

3. Products and preorders

Availability labels distinguish stocked, out-of-stock, coming-soon and preorder products. A preorder may have a later or estimated delivery date. Product appearance, compatibility and lead-time information must be checked before ordering. Wobli will contact the customer if a material delay or compatibility issue affects the order.

4. Accounts and conversations

Use accurate contact information, protect your password and use only your own account. Do not send spam, unlawful material or information you are not entitled to share. Questions and replies are private to the account holder and site administration. Avoid including payment details or unnecessary sensitive information.

5. Installation and Custom Lab

Installation and Custom Lab forms create requests for review. A request, conversation or estimate is not a confirmed booking or final quote. Scope, compatibility, price and timing must be separately agreed before work begins. Clearly personalised or made-to-order items may not be eligible for voluntary cancellation once production has started, subject to mandatory consumer rights.

6. Cancellations, returns and refunds

The cancellation, return and refund process will be published before online orders are enabled.

7. Return condition and costs

For an approved voluntary return, the product must be unused, complete and safely packaged unless it is being returned because of a defect or Wobli error. The customer pays return shipping when informed and permitted by applicable law. No product should be sent back until return instructions have been provided.

8. Privacy and updates

See the Privacy Policy for account, order, delivery, payment and message data. The policy version accepted at checkout and its acceptance time are stored with the order. Contact details and the legal operator must be verified before these notices are treated as final.

Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

Draft · Updated 31 August 2026

Details to complete before public launch.

Confirm the responsible operator’s legal name, postal address and working privacy contact, the hosting and backup providers and countries, any international-transfer safeguards, and data-retention periods. This draft describes the current application, not a verified live hosting setup.

1. Who is responsible?

Wobli is the project name. The responsible person or legal entity, contact details and full postal address will be specified before launch.

2. Information processed

Account data includes your email address and a hashed password. Profile data and conversations can include information you submit, timestamps and read status. Session and login-attempt records support account security.

3. Why it is used

This information is used to authenticate accounts, take and fulfil orders, process payments, provide order updates, answer messages and service requests, send transactional emails, prevent misuse and meet legal record-keeping duties.

4. Cookies and browser storage

The app uses login cookies with an eight-hour lifetime. Your cart and whether you have seen the preorder introduction are saved in your browser’s local storage until cleared. Clearing storage can reset your cart and preferences; blocking login cookies prevents signed-in features. No advertising or analytics trackers are included in the current application code. Any tracking added later needs a fresh review and updated notice.

5. Access and service providers

The account holder and authorized administrators can access the relevant account and conversation information. Production service providers, locations, safeguards and backup arrangements will be documented before launch.

6. Retention and deletion

Account, order, policy-acceptance and conversation records are retained while needed to provide the service and comply with accounting, tax, warranty, dispute and other legal obligations. Account deletion does not require deletion of records that Wobli must lawfully retain. Exact retention and backup-deletion periods must be established before launch.

7. Your choices and rights

You can edit your email, password and optional profile details in Settings and Personal details. Depending on applicable law, you may request access, correction or deletion of your personal data, subject to legal exceptions. Use the verified operator contact once confirmed; identity verification may be needed to protect your information. You may also contact the relevant data-protection authority, including Switzerland’s FDPIC.

8. Changes

This notice must be reviewed when hosting, payments, email delivery, analytics or other data-processing features change.
