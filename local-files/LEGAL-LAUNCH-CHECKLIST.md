# Legal notices — items to complete before launch

The Terms & Conditions and Privacy Policy in the website are drafts for the current preview. They are not a legal compliance certification or final terms of sale.

Confirm:
- Legal operator’s full name/entity, full postal address, and working contact email.
- Hosting and backup providers, data locations, recipients, international transfers and safeguards where relevant.
- Retention periods, deletion procedures and handling of privacy requests.
- Target sales countries and applicable consumer rules.
- Before checkout: price/tax disclosures, shipping, payment, contract formation, preorder delays, cancellation/returns, defect/warranty handling and custom-work conditions.
- Before adding email or analytics: provider details and updated privacy/storage disclosures.

Have the completed notices reviewed for the actual business before accepting orders. No mandatory registration acceptance checkbox has been added for these unfinished draft terms.
